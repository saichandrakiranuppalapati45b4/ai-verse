# AI Verse Club Portal
## Project Overview

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Project Name:** AI Verse Club Portal

**Organization:** AI & Data Science (AI&DS) Department

**Project Type:** Full Stack Web Application

**Development Status:** Planning Phase

---

# 1. Introduction

The AI Verse Club Portal is a centralized web platform designed to manage and showcase all activities of the AI Verse Club under the AI & Data Science Department.

The portal serves two primary purposes:

- **Public Website** for visitors to learn about the club, upcoming events, achievements, team members, and gallery.
- **Management Dashboard** for faculty coordinators and student organizers to efficiently manage club operations.

The goal is to eliminate manual management of club activities by providing a modern, secure, and easy-to-use digital platform.

---

# 2. Vision

To create a modern, intelligent, and scalable digital platform that becomes the official online presence of the AI Verse Club while simplifying event management, communication, and collaboration among students and faculty.

---

# 3. Mission

- Provide a professional online identity for AI Verse.
- Digitize club management processes.

- Showcase club achievements.
- Maintain a centralized repository for all club activities.
